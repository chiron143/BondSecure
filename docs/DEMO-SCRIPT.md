# Demo script (about 4:20, under the 5-minute limit)

One headline job: getting the bond back. Move-in day is the follow-on that makes the claim stronger.
Sample data is used for the claim (operator "Sample Rooms"); the move-in video is your real room.

## Before you record (10 minutes)

1. **Set up the screen.** Laptop Chrome → https://bondsecure.vercel.app → press `Ctrl+Shift+M` (device toolbar) → choose an iPhone size. Record with Loom, or Windows `Win+Alt+R`, with your microphone on. Close other tabs and turn notifications off.
2. **Make the move-in report off camera** (so it exists for step 4): open `/move-in`, use your room video (talk while filming), set the **move-in date to 3 February 2026**, confirm all lines, and download the PDF. Keep the PDF and the video file handy.
3. **Have the three sample documents ready:** `docs/demo-documents/1-receipt.png`, `2-terms.png`, `3-email.png`.
4. **Open a fresh tab** for the recording (so the form starts empty), and rehearse once.

Backups if the AI is busy on the day: "Show a demo result instead" in the receipt reader, and `/move-in?demo=1`. Both are labelled as demo on screen.

## The script

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home page | "Meet Mei, an international student from China studying in Sydney, based on my own case. Mei paid an $802 deposit. The terms promised it back in 15 days. Seven weeks later: 'still being processed'. Last year about 6,800 bond disputes reached the NSW tribunal, and most people face them with no lawyer. Mei doesn't know which law applies, if they've broken it, or what to send." |
| 0:25 | Tap **Get my bond back**. Add the 3 sample documents, tap **Read 3 files** | "Mei drops in the receipt, the terms and the operator's email. AI reads them: the company's legal name, the $802, the date it was paid, the 15-day promise, the move-out date. And it shows the exact words it read, so Mei can check every one." |
| 0:50 | Tap **Use these in my answers**. Upload the move-in report PDF. Answer the rest: NSW, a company that runs rooms, 5 or more people, no bond number | "Mei confirms them, adds the move-in report made on day one, and answers a few plain questions only a renter can answer." |
| 1:10 | **Show me where I stand** | "Here's the important part, and it's deliberately not AI. Tested rules work out the law: it's a boarding house, so the Boarding Houses Act says 14 days. Mei's refund is 36 days overdue." |
| 1:30 | Scroll to **How we decided** | "And it shows its working: the rule, the official source, the date we checked it. If Mei is ever unsure, it says so and points to a free legal centre." |
| 1:45 | **Read this in** → 简体中文 (Chinese), then back to English | "Mei can read all of this in Chinese. The letter stays in English, because that's what the operator and the tribunal read." |
| 2:00 | Scroll to **Your letter**, then **Download my claim pack**. Open the PDF at **Already there when I moved in** | "Mei gets a formal demand that cites the law and the deadline, and a claim pack: the timeline, an NCAT kit if they still don't pay, and photos of damage that was already there on move-in day." |
| 2:30 | Scroll up, **Change my answers** → **Try an example case** → **Not sure how many people live there** | "Real cases are messy. If Mei isn't sure how many people live there, it doesn't guess. Fewer than 5 would make Mei a lodger with no fixed deadline, so it shows both answers and says to check with a free legal service first." |
| 2:55 | **Moving in** → the **Before you film** box | "Those photos came from the follow-on: move-in day. Film the room for two minutes, and talk while you film." |
| 3:05 | Upload your room video → progress panel (**cut or speed up the wait**) | "AI watches every second and listens to the narration. It takes a minute or two, and we chose accuracy over speed." |
| 3:15 | Review screen: scroll the stills, confirm, **Create my report** | "This is my real room. It found the blistered paint at 21 seconds, the mould at 28, the peeled paint by the switch. Each still is from my own video, and I confirm every line. The PDF records the video's fingerprint, and months later it drops straight into a claim." |
| 3:45 | Home page, scroll to the tester quotes | "Four international students tried it cold this weekend. The report found every mark they pointed at, and all four would send the letter. Where they got stuck, we fixed it." |
| 4:05 | Hero again | "Bond Secure. From 'where do I even start?' to a formal demand that cites the law, with proof, in about ten minutes. Legal information, not legal advice. bondsecure.vercel.app." |

## After recording

- Trim the AI wait (or speed it up 4x) and any dead air. Check the total is under 5:00.
- Upload as **unlisted** YouTube or Loom; open the link in a private window to check judges can watch it.
